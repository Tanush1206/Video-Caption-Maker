Privacy

VideoCaptionMaker runs on your own computer. It has no servers and no accounts, and it sends nothing to its developer: no telemetry, no crash reports, no usage statistics. Your videos, captions and exports stay on your computer.

What leaves your computer, and when

ToWhenWhat is sent
GitHubInstalling, and vcm updateRequests for the install scripts and release files
GitHub Container Registry and Docker HubInstalling, and vcm updateRequests for the app's images and the database, queue and search images it uses
Hugging FaceThe first time each model is neededRequests for the model files, with no account or token. Once a model is downloaded, it isn't contacted again for it
GitHub (fonts)The first time you pick a font from the Google Fonts libraryA request for that font file
Google, Gemini APIOnly if you add your own Gemini key in SettingsCaption text only: up to 25 lines at a time for a translation, or your question and up to 6 matching captions. Never the video or its sound

Each of these services sees your computer's IP address when it is contacted, under its own privacy policy. Nothing else leaves your computer. Without a Gemini key, once the models are downloaded, captioning, translation, editing, export and search work offline.

Gemini, if you add a key

The key is your own, so Google's Gemini API terms apply to you directly. Among them:

  • Free keys: Google may use what you send (here, caption text) to improve its products, and people at Google may read it. With a paid key, it doesn't use it to improve its products.
  • Age: you must be 18 or over to use the Gemini API.
  • Europe: in the European Economic Area, Switzerland and the United Kingdom, only paid keys may be used.

For anything private, use a paid key, or none: the app then translates with its own model, on your computer.

This website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub records visitors' IP addresses in its server logs under its privacy statement. It counts visits with GoatCounter: which page was opened, and clicks on the Copy buttons and the Windows/macOS/Linux switch, as totals. GoatCounter sets no cookies and, by its own description, stores no IP addresses or other personal data. Nothing else on the page sends data anywhere.

Your videos

You need the right to use the videos you caption. If you caption videos of other people for work, you are responsible for that use under the law that applies to you (in India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act). The app itself collects no personal data on any server, because it has none.

The app is provided as is, with no warranty. Captions and translations can be wrong; check them before you publish them.
